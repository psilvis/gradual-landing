Six timed tests you can do at home. The benchmark compares your strength and mobility balance to calculate your age percentiles.

Strength, mobility, control

Each test is backed by population data, and insights are personalized based on your activity levels, sleep and labwork data.

Push Push-ups to failure. Upper body strength endurance.
Legs Wall sit held to failure. Lower body endurance.
Core Forearm plank. Trunk stability under fatigue.
Mobility Sit and rise from the floor without using your hands.
Balance Single leg stand, eyes closed. Neural control. It's harder than it sounds.
Grip Dead hang from a bar. Grip strength tracks whole body strength.
